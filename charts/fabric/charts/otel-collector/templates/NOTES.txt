The exporter queue survives Collector restarts on retained PVC storage.
This provides bounded at-least-once transport, NOT exactly-once delivery:
ambiguous acknowledgements can produce duplicates and permanent errors,
queue/disk exhaustion, storage loss, or pre-queue policy drops can still
lose telemetry. The queue is not the authoritative evidence store.
{{- else }}
WARNING: exporter queue state is memory-only and is lost with the pod.
Enable exporter.sendingQueue.persistence for restart-safe buffering.
{{- end }}
