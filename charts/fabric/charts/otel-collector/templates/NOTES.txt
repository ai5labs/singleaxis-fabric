WARNING: fabricpolicy bundlePath is set to {{ .Values.fabric.policy.bundlePath }}
but no ConfigMap is mounted there (fabric.policy.bundleConfigMap is
empty). The volume renders as an emptyDir and will stay EMPTY unless
you provision Rego into it out of band. An empty bundle makes
fabricpolicy FAIL CLOSED: every record AND span in the pipeline is
dropped, silently destroying your telemetry.

Fix:
  --set otel-collector.fabric.policy.bundleConfigMap=<configmap-with-rego>
