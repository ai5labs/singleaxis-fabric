=====================================================================
WARNING: NO TELEMETRY BACKEND IS CONFIGURED.

`otel-collector.exporter.endpoint` is empty, so the collector exports
nowhere. Spans and logs are written to the collector pod's stdout via
the OTel `debug` exporter and nothing else. Read them with:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name=otel-collector -f

Pod stdout is not durable, is not queryable, and is NOT an audit
trail — it survives only as long as the pod and its log rotation. Do
not make a retention or compliance claim on this install.

Point the collector at a real OTLP/HTTP backend:

  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} <chart> -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    --set otel-collector.exporter.endpoint=http://your-collector:4318

Backend examples (Datadog, Honeycomb, your own collector chain) are
in charts/fabric/charts/otel-collector/values.yaml.
=====================================================================
